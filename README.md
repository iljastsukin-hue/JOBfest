# JOBfest 🇪🇪

### Töö lähedal. Abi lähedal.
**Local jobs. Local help.**

JOBfest is a mobile marketplace designed to connect people who need help with everyday tasks with people who are willing to complete those tasks for payment.

Our mission is to make finding local help and earning money through small jobs simple, fast, and accessible throughout Estonia.

---

## 💡 Project Overview

JOBfest is a location-based platform where users can post jobs, discover available tasks nearby, communicate with each other, and arrange work.

The platform is designed for both individuals and service providers.

A single user account can operate in two roles:
- **Customer** — someone who posts a job and looks for help.
- **Worker** — someone who searches for jobs and completes them.

Users can switch between these roles whenever needed.

## 🗺️ Core Concept — Interactive Map

The main screen of JOBfest will feature an interactive map covering the whole of Estonia.

Users will be able to:
- View available jobs directly on the map.
- See job prices displayed on map markers.
- Search by city, address, or location.
- Set a preferred search radius.
- Filter jobs by category.
- Switch between map and list views.
- Discover jobs in both urban and rural areas.

The goal is to make nearby work opportunities easy to find.

## 🔨 Job Categories

The platform will support a wide range of everyday tasks and services, including:

- Home and office cleaning
- Window cleaning
- Moving and carrying
- Furniture assembly
- Repairs and maintenance
- Gardening and landscaping
- Painting and finishing
- Construction work
- Concrete and earthworks
- Fence installation
- Deliveries and errands
- Waste removal
- Other custom jobs

Users will also be able to describe tasks using free text.

## 👤 User Features

### Customer
- Create and publish job listings.
- Add a description, location, category, and price.
- Set a preferred completion date.
- Review worker applications.
- Communicate with workers.
- Mark jobs as completed.
- Rate and review workers.

### Worker
- Browse available jobs.
- Search and filter tasks.
- Apply for jobs.
- Communicate with customers.
- Track job status.
- Receive ratings and reviews.

## 💬 Communication

JOBfest will include an internal messaging system that allows customers and workers to communicate directly.

Users will be able to discuss job details, agree on arrangements, and receive updates.

## ⭐ Trust and Safety

Planned trust and safety features:
- User profiles.
- Ratings and reviews.
- Job status tracking.
- Reporting and complaint system.
- Moderation tools.
- Basic fraud prevention measures.
